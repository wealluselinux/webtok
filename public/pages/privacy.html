Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This explains what Web Tok collects, why, and what you can do about it. Web Tok is run by an individual site owner. Contact: meganlayow440@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email, username, display name, bio, profile picture, and your password (stored only as a scrambled "hash", never as plain text).
  • Things you post: videos, captions, hashtags, comments, likes, follows, and messages to friends.
  • Activity on the site: which videos you watched and liked, so we can suggest videos on your For You page.
  • Moderation records: the date you accepted these policies and notes about actions taken on an account (for example a restriction or suspension).
  • Technical data: our hosting provider keeps normal server logs such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To run the site: log you in, show your videos, comments, and messages.
  • To recommend videos based on hashtags of videos you watch and like.
  • To keep the site safe, handle reports, and enforce our Terms.
  • To send in-browser notifications about new messages, if you turn them on.

We don't sell your personal information and we don't show ads.

Who can see what

  • Public: your username, display name, bio, profile picture, videos, comments, likes, and who you follow.
  • Friends only: your messages (between you and the friend). They are stored on our servers without end-to-end encryption, and the site owner and admins may be able to access them for safety or legal reasons.
  • Only you and the site team: your email address and your viewing history.

Services that help us run Web Tok

  • Render hosts the website.
  • Turso stores the database (accounts, comments, messages).
  • Cloudflare R2 stores uploaded videos and profile pictures.

These providers handle data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

Cookies and browser storage

Web Tok uses your browser's local storage (not advertising cookies) to keep you signed in, remember your theme, and remember your notification choices.

How long we keep things

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account in profile settings, your profile, videos, comments, likes, follows, and messages are deleted from the live site. Short-lived backups and server logs may remain for a limited time.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile or delete your videos and comments at any time.
  • Delete your account in profile settings.
  • Turn browser notifications off in your browser's site settings.
  • Email us to ask for a copy of your information or for help with a privacy question.

Children

Web Tok is not for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will remove it.

Security

We use secure connections (HTTPS), store passwords as hashes, and limit who can access the database. No website can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for big changes, may ask you to accept it again.
